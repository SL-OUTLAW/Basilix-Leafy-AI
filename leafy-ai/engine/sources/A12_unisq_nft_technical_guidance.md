# Aquaponic System Design Report - NFT Technical Guidance

## Nutrient film technique

Nutrient film technique is a hydroponic method in which a shallow flow of nutrient solution passes through growing channels.

The roots of plants are exposed to the moving nutrient solution while also receiving oxygen from the air.

NFT can be suitable for short-cycle crops including leafy greens and herbs such as basil.

## NFT channel design

The design of an NFT channel affects the movement of nutrient solution and the condition of plant roots.

The channel should provide a consistent and shallow nutrient film.

Channel grade influences the speed and depth of nutrient-solution flow.

An appropriate slope is required to maintain reliable movement of solution through the channel without creating excessive water depth.

## Nutrient-solution flow

Adequate nutrient-solution flow is needed to supply water and nutrients to the crop.

Flow must be sufficient to prevent drying of plant roots.

Excessively high flow can increase turbulence and may interfere with stable root-zone conditions.

The flow rate should therefore be matched to the channel design and crop requirements.

## Root-zone conditions

NFT relies on a balance between nutrient-solution contact and root exposure to air.

The shallow nutrient film allows part of the root system to remain exposed to air while another part is supplied with nutrient solution.

Maintaining suitable root-zone oxygen availability is important for healthy plant growth.

## Filtration

Filtration can be used to remove suspended particles from recirculating water.

Particles can accumulate in pumps, pipes and channels.

Appropriate filtration can reduce the risk of blockages and improve the reliability of water flow.

Filtration requirements depend on the design of the system and the quality of the incoming water.

## Recirculation

NFT systems can operate as recirculating systems in which nutrient solution is collected and returned to a reservoir.

Recirculation can reduce water use and allow nutrient solution to be reused.

A recirculating system requires monitoring of nutrient-solution condition and reliable pumping.

## Pumping

The pump must provide sufficient flow to distribute nutrient solution through all channels.

Pump selection should consider required flow, head height and system resistance.

A pump that is too small may not provide adequate flow to all channels.

Pump reliability is particularly important because interruption of nutrient flow can rapidly affect plants in an NFT system.

## System layout

NFT systems should be designed so that nutrient solution can move consistently through each growing channel.

Reservoirs, pumps, distribution lines and return lines should be arranged to reduce unnecessary pressure losses.

Access to pumps, filters and reservoirs should be considered during system design and maintenance.

## Monitoring

Important variables in a recirculating NFT system include nutrient-solution level, flow, pH, electrical conductivity and water temperature.

Monitoring can help identify failures or changes in system conditions.

A sudden change in flow or nutrient-solution level may indicate a pump, pipe or blockage problem.

## System hygiene

Shared nutrient solution can provide a pathway for pathogens to move through a recirculating system.

Regular cleaning and maintenance of reservoirs, pumps, filters and channels can reduce contamination risks.

System hygiene should form part of routine NFT management.

## Basil relevance

NFT is suitable for short-cycle leafy crops and herbs, including basil.

The system-design principles described here can help explain flow, channel slope, filtration and root-zone requirements in an NFT system.

The report is primarily an aquaponics design document rather than a basil-specific hydroponic production guide.

Its technical principles should therefore be used as supporting NFT knowledge rather than as project-specific basil operating limits.
