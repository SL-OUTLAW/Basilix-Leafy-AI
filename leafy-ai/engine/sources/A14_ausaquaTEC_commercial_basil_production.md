# Commercial Hydroponics - Basil Production

## Commercial basil production

Sweet basil can be produced commercially using controlled-environment hydroponic systems.

Commercial hydroponic basil production can use greenhouse structures to provide greater control over temperature, humidity, airflow and plant growth conditions.

## Nutrient film technique

Nutrient film technique is a common hydroponic production method for commercial basil.

In NFT systems, a shallow stream of nutrient solution flows through growing channels and passes over the plant roots.

The system recirculates nutrient solution between the growing channels and a reservoir.

## Plant spacing

Plant spacing affects crop density, airflow and the amount of growing space available to each plant.

Spacing should be selected according to cultivar, plant size and the intended harvest stage.

Very close spacing can reduce airflow around foliage and may increase humidity within the crop canopy.

## Greenhouse temperature

Temperature is an important factor in commercial basil production.

Basil grows best under warm conditions.

Greenhouse temperatures should be managed according to crop requirements and local environmental conditions.

Temperature should be considered together with humidity and airflow.

## Humidity

Relative humidity affects plant transpiration and disease risk.

High humidity can increase the likelihood of disease development when foliage remains wet for extended periods.

Greenhouse ventilation and air movement can help manage humidity.

## Airflow

Adequate airflow through the basil crop can improve environmental uniformity.

Air movement can help reduce areas of stagnant humid air around the foliage.

Good airflow can also support leaf drying after irrigation or condensation.

## Irrigation and nutrient solution

Hydroponic basil depends on nutrient solution for water and mineral nutrients.

The nutrient solution should be monitored and maintained according to the requirements of the crop and growing system.

Important measurements can include electrical conductivity and pH.

Water quality can influence nutrient-solution management.

## Recirculation

Recirculating nutrient solution can improve water and fertiliser efficiency.

Because the same solution is repeatedly returned to the crop, regular monitoring is required.

Changes in nutrient concentration, pH and EC can occur as plants absorb water and nutrients.

## Plant health

Regular monitoring of basil plants is important in commercial production.

Leaves should be inspected for signs of disease, nutrient problems, pests and environmental stress.

Changes in plant appearance should be considered together with environmental and nutrient-solution measurements.

## Harvesting and production management

Commercial basil production requires coordination of plant growth, spacing, irrigation, environmental conditions and harvesting.

Harvest timing influences crop quality and subsequent plant growth.

Production systems should be managed to maintain suitable conditions throughout the crop cycle.
