TARGET IMAGE
Sweet basil in the Leafy AI hydroponic farm.

Return JSON only.
Follow the supplied schema exactly.

REFERENCE IMAGES
EARLY, MIDDLE and MATURE reference images may be supplied for the same camera.
Use them only to calibrate relative plant size and growth stage.

A PREVIOUS IMAGE may also be supplied.
Use it only for visual growth trend.
If no previous image is supplied, growth.trend must be insufficient_history and history_used must be false.

HEALTH
Use visible evidence from the target image only.

health.status
healthy: no meaningful visible stress
stress_suspected: meaningful visible stress is present
uncertain: the image is not reliable enough to decide

For dryness_wilt, overwatering_like and nutrient_deficiency_like return:
presence: none, possible, clear or uncertain
severity: none, mild, moderate, severe or uncertain
distribution: none, localized, widespread or uncertain
observations: visible signs only

If presence is none:
severity must be none
distribution must be none
observations must be empty

Dryness/wilt observations can include:

desiccated_tissue

Use downy_mildew_like only when visible features are consistent with downy mildew.
Use powdery_mildew_like only when powdery white surface growth or patches are visible.

Do not force a disease type when evidence is weak.

PLANT SIZE
Return small, medium, large or uncertain.
Use the same-camera references when supplied.
Judge the whole visible crop, not one foreground leaf.
This is relative image-space size, not centimetres or physical height.
reference_match is the closest reference stage.

CANOPY AND CROWDING
coverage_percent =
visible basil foliage pixels / all target-image pixels * 100

Count basil foliage only.
Do not count channels, trays, pipes, walls, lights, labels or empty background.

visible_gaps:
many: large open areas remain between plant groups
some: clear gaps remain while the canopy is filling
few: most of the visible crop area is closed

overlap:
low: little visible leaf overlap
moderate: regular overlap
high: dense overlap across much of the crop

crowding_state:
not_crowded: many gaps and low overlap
moderate: some gaps or moderate overlap
crowded: few gaps and high overlap
uncertain: the image cannot support a reliable crowding result

GROWTH
stage:
early
middle
mature
uncertain

Use the same-camera references when supplied.
Judge the whole crop view.

early: separated plant groups and more exposed channels
middle: greater foliage mass and row filling with clear gaps still present
mature: strongest canopy closure and dense overlap

If a previous image is supplied, compare only compatible views from the same camera.
trend:
increasing: visibly more developed target crop
stable: no meaningful visible change
decreasing: visibly less foliage or apparent crop development
uncertain: comparison is not reliable
